chess_arm - a 6-joint (RRRRRR) robot arm plays chess in a 3D simulator. Gemini chooses the robot's moves.

Run:  python main.py            (opens the window; chess_arm is the default feature)

Gemini card
  API key      typed here (kept in memory only) or taken from the GEMINI_API_KEY environment variable.
  Connection   Standard = one generateContent request per move. Live = a WebSocket session (needs the websockets
               package); fast, but the models answer in voice, so the move is read from the transcription.
               Changing it clears the model box: press "Refresh models" again for the matching list.
  Model        press "Refresh models" to list every model of your key that can generate text, then pick one.
               The box is editable: you can also type any model name. Nothing is hard-coded.
Game card
  Gemini plays Black / White / Both (Gemini vs Gemini) / None (manual).
  Click        click one of your pieces (side to move): its legal squares light up (gold = move, red = capture);
               click a lit square and the arm plays it. A pawn reaching the last rank becomes a queen.
               Click another piece to change the selection, click anywhere else to cancel.
  Your move    UCI (e2e4, e7e8q) or SAN (Nf3, O-O), then Play or Enter.
  Gemini move now   ask Gemini for the side to move.
  New game     reset board and arm; the side picked in "Gemini plays" ends up nearest to the arm.
Arm card
  Speed slider and live joint angles. Mouse in the 3D view: drag = orbit, wheel = zoom, double-click = reset view.

If Gemini answers with an illegal move it is asked again (3 tries), then the game waits for you.
