chess_arm - a 6-joint (RRRRRR) robot arm plays chess or xiangqi in a 3D simulator. An engine chooses the robot's moves; Gemini Live chats about the game.

Run:  python main.py            (opens the window; chess_arm is the default feature)

Header
  Game         Chess or Xiangqi (Cờ tướng). Switching puts that board on the table in its initial position.
               Xiangqi: 9 x 10 crossings, river, palaces, 32 flat discs with the real names (red 帥仕相傌俥炮兵,
               black 將士象馬車砲卒) drawn from a system font (Microsoft JhengHei / YaHei).
               Full rules: legal moves, check, flying general, horse leg, elephant eye, river, cannon screen.
               The game ends when the side to move has no legal move (that side loses). No repetition rule.
               The engine is a 3-ply material search plus a little activity; xiangqi moves are announced in the
               traditional Vietnamese notation (pháo 2 bình 5: files counted from the mover's right).
               "Robot plays White" reads "Red" in xiangqi. Moves are typed in ICCS: h2e2 = from h2 to e2
               (files a-i left to right, ranks 0-9 from the red back rank). Click-to-move works as in chess.
Gemini companion card
  The robot's moves come from a built-in engine (chess: core/chess_search.py, xiangqi: core/xiangqi_search.py, about
  2-4 s per move). Gemini Live is only the companion: it says the robot's move as its own ("pháo 2 bình 5 ... để xem
  bạn làm gì nhé"), comments on your moves and chats. It never picks a move.
  API key      typed here (kept in memory only) or taken from the GEMINI_API_KEY environment variable.
  Model        press "Refresh models" to list the models of your key that support the Live API, then pick one.
               The box is editable. Models named "...extended-thinking" work but think before every sentence.
  Connect companion   opens one Live session for the whole game (needs the websockets package). The game itself
               also runs without it. Closing the session and connecting again restarts the chat; the new session is
               told the current position.
  Mic          streams your voice to Gemini while on (16 kHz mono). It is muted while Gemini speaks, so use
               headphones if you want to talk over it. Your words and its answers appear in the chat box.
  Chat box     type a message and press Enter: Gemini answers aloud and in the chat box.
  Gemini only knows the game through messages the software sends after each move (move in words, facts, the board).

Game card
  Robot (engine) plays Black / White / Both (engine vs engine, waits for the companion to finish talking) / None.
  Click        click one of your pieces (side to move): its legal squares light up (gold = move, red = capture);
               click a lit square and the arm plays it. A pawn reaching the last rank becomes a queen.
               Click another piece to change the selection, click anywhere else to cancel.
  Your move    UCI (e2e4, e7e8q) or SAN (Nf3, O-O), then Play or Enter.
  Robot move now    let the engine play the side to move.
  New game     reset board and arm; the side picked in "Gemini plays" ends up nearest to the arm.
Arm card
  Speed slider and live joint angles. Mouse in the 3D view: drag = orbit, wheel = zoom, double-click = reset view.
